⚠ Stay safe: spot a scam

Real employers NEVER ask you to pay to get hired, buy gift cards, or cash a check and send money back. If a “job” asks for that — or your bank login, or payment for “equipment” up front — it is a scam. Stop and ask your local workforce center to look at the posting with you. That help is free.

For whoever runs this tool (workforce center, library, nonprofit, family). The admin vault is encrypted in this browser with AES-256 — your password is never stored. Unlocking lets you edit the announcement, job sites, county notes, private admin notes, and data tools.

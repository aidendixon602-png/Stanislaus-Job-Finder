⚠ Stay safe: spot a scam

Real employers NEVER ask you to pay to get hired, buy gift cards, or cash a check and send money back. If a “job” asks for any of that — or wants your bank login, full SSN by text/email before an interview, or payment for “equipment” up front — it is a scam. Stop, don’t send money or personal info, and report it. When in doubt, ask Stanislaus Workforce Development (above) to look at the posting with you — that help is free.

  • Apply through the employer’s real website or a site in the directory above.
  • Be careful with jobs that only contact you by text/Telegram/WhatsApp and offer high pay for very easy work.
  • Never deposit a check from a “new employer” and forward part of it — the check will bounce and you’ll owe the bank.
